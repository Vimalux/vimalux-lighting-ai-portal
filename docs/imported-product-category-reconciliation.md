# Imported product/category reconciliation

For imported technical projects, a stored proposed luminaire must remain compatible with the selected luminaire category.

- URBAN / Arredo urbano cannot keep STREET products such as MANTA/MAKO/ZETA.
- STREET / Stradale cannot keep URBAN products such as OPERA/RETRO.
- Compatible existing selections are preserved.
- If an imported selection is incompatible, Intelligence chooses the closest-wattage compatible active catalogue product.
- This reconciliation runs after confirmed re-import and once after load to repair already-imported projects created before the rule was enforced.

CRM identity, Business Case identity, commercial assumptions and offer/history are not changed by this reconciliation.
