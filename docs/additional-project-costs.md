# Additional project costs

Project-specific costs are stored on each Business Case in `additionalCosts`.

- `capex`: one-off project investment items such as poles, civil works, extra cabling or labour.
- `opex_annual`: recurring annual items such as services, licences or additional maintenance.

Each row stores quantity, unit, internal unit cost, customer unit sales price and note. CAPEX rows feed project CAPEX and financing. Annual OPEX rows feed recurring customer payment and direct project cost.
